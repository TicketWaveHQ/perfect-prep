## What we do

**Perfect Prep** is a small, family-run halal kitchen serving **Yorkshire & Teesside**. Halal is how we
source; good cooking is what we sell — and everyone's welcome at the table.

- 🍱 **Weekly meal prep** — five fresh bowls in rotation, cooked each weekend. Order by **Saturday**,
  delivered **Sunday**, ready for the week. Most bowls £8–£12; weekly bundles work out cheaper.
- 🎉 **Catering** — parties, weddings, BBQs, corporate days, music-video shoots and casual linkups,
  **10 to 300 covers**. 100% halal, delivered hot. From £8/head, quote within 24h.
- ✅ **100% halal** — halal-certified meat, no alcohol in marinades, cooked fresh in-house.

## Where we deliver

Across **Yorkshire & Teesside** every Sunday — Leeds, Bradford, Wakefield, Dewsbury, Huddersfield,
Halifax, Harrogate and out to Teesside (LS · BD · HD · WF · HX · HG · YO · TS · DL · SR).
Outside the usual radius? DM us — we sometimes extend.

## How to order

DM **[@_perfectprep_](https://www.instagram.com/_perfectprep_/)** on Instagram with the meals you want
(or your headcount for catering) and your delivery postcode. We confirm your total and your Sunday
slot — pay on confirmation, no upfront commitment. Recurring weekly plans available; cancel any time.
